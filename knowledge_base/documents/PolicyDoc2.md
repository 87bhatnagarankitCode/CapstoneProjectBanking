# CRED LENDING OPERATIONS - FEES, OPERATIONS, AND ASSET CLASSIFICATION (v1.0.0)
**Document Ref:** CRED/OPS/REPAY-2026/V1
**Classification:** Public-Facing Operational Guidelines

## SECTION 1: CREDIT CARD FEE STRUCTURE & MINIMUM BALANCE REQUIREMENTS
* **Annual Membership Fees:** The CRED Premium Credit Card carries an annual membership fee of ₹2,500, which is waived if the total annual spend crosses ₹1,500,000. Finance charges on revolving balances are calculated at a monthly rate of 3.5% (42% per annum).
* **Minimum Balance & Account Rules:** For CRED integrated digital wallets and neo-banking partner suites, a minimum balance requirement of ₹10,000 must be maintained at the end of each monthly cycle. Failure to maintain this attracts a tiered penalty starting at ₹250 per month.

## SECTION 2: BOUNCE PENALTY, DRIFT, AND DEGRADATION
* **Technical & Financial Bounces:** Any NACH/ECS mandate return triggered by insufficient funds will incur a flat systemic penalty of ₹500 + 18% GST (Total ₹590) per automated presentation failure. Non-refundable penal interest compounding of 2% per month is applied on overdue balances.
* **CIBIL Degradation Alert:** If a bounced EMI remains unresolved for more than 72 hours from the presentation window, an automated negative report packet is pushed to TransUnion CIBIL and Experian. This typically results in an immediate structural drop of 15 to 30 points in the member's credit score.

## SECTION 3: FRAUD-DISPUTE RESOLUTION & ACCOUNT-CLOSURE PROCESS
* **Fraud-Dispute Resolution Process:** Any unauthorized transactional activity or suspect application must be flagged within 48 hours to the CRED Fraud Monitoring Cell. Investigation pipelines take up to 7 working days, during which the disputed amount is placed under a temporary credit hold.
* **Account-Closure Process:** Members can initiate an account-closure process directly via the CRED app after clearing all outstanding balances, unbilled dues, and pending interest. A digitally signed No Dues Certificate (NDC) is issued within 3 business days via registered email.
