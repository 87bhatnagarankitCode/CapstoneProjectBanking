# HFDC BANK - CREDIT RISK AND RETAIL LENDING ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA (v1.0.0)
**Document Ref:** HFDC/RLL/CR-2026/RE-09
**Classification:** Internal Confidental - For AI Retrieval Only

## SECTION 1: PERSONAL LOAN DISBURSEMENT MATRIX (UNSECURED LENDING)
### 1.1 Demographics & Age Brackets
* **Entry Threshold:** The applicant must be a minimum of 21 calendar years of age at the time of initial file login.
* **Exit Ceiling:** Maximum age is capped strictly at 60 years at the time of projected Loan Maturity for salaried individuals, and 65 years for self-employed professionals/proprietors.

### 1.2 Income Verification & FOIR (Fixed Income to Obligation Ratio)
* **Tier-1 Metro Locations (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Pune):** Minimum net monthly disposable salary credited via corporate banking channels must be ≥ ₹25,000.
* **Tier-2/Tier-3 Locations:** Minimum net monthly salary must be ≥ ₹20,000.
* **FOIR Cap:** Total monthly debt obligations (current EMIs + proposed HFDC EMI) must not exceed 50% of the net verified income. For individuals with net salary > ₹1,00,000, the FOIR cap can be dynamically extended to 60% upon Credit Manager approval.

### 1.3 Bureau Score (CIBIL Verification)
* **Prime Pool:** CIBIL Score ≥ 750 qualifies for the baseline interest rate structure.
* **Sub-Prime Guardrail:** CIBIL Scores between 700 and 749 attract a mandatory risk-premium loading of +50 bps (0.50%) on the interest rate.
* **Hard Rejection:** Any bureau file showing a score < 700, or a "Settled"/"Written-Off" tag on active accounts within the last 24 months, will trigger an automated systemic rejection.

## SECTION 2: SECURED HOME LENDING FRAMEWORK
### 2.1 Loan-To-Value (LTV) Ratios
* For asset valuations up to ₹30 Lakhs: Maximum permissible LTV is capped at 80% of the technical-legal valuation report.
* For asset valuations between ₹30 Lakhs and ₹75 Lakhs: Maximum LTV is capped at 75%.
* For luxury segments (> ₹75 Lakhs): Maximum LTV is restricted to 70%.

### 2.2 Collateral Legal Clearances
* The underlying property must have an unencumbered title. 
* Agricultural land conversions (NA plots) require a certified Collector's NOC. Mixed-use commercial/residential structures cannot be leveraged under standard retail home loan books.
