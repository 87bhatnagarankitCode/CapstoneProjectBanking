# CRED LENDING OPERATIONS - RETAIL LENDING ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA (v1.0.0)
**Document Ref:** CRED/LEND/EL-2026/V1
**Classification:** Internal Confidential - For AI Retrieval Only

## SECTION 1: CRED PERSONAL LOAN ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
* **Age Brackets:** The applicant must be a minimum of 21 calendar years of age at the time of initial application. Maximum age is capped strictly at 60 years at the time of projected Loan Maturity for salaried members.
* **Income Verification & FOIR:** For Tier-1 metro locations, minimum net monthly disposable salary credited via corporate banking channels must be ≥ ₹25,000. Total monthly debt obligations (current EMIs + proposed CRED EMI) must not exceed a FOIR cap of 50% of verified income.
* **Credit Score Impact Factors & Bureau Guardrail:** CRED members must maintain a TransUnion CIBIL Score ≥ 750 to qualify for baseline interest rates. CIBIL scores between 700 and 749 attract a risk loading premium of +50 bps, while any score < 700 results in automated systemic rejection.

## SECTION 2: SECURED HOME LENDING FRAMEWORK
* **Loan-To-Value (LTV) Ratios:** For property asset valuations up to ₹30 Lakhs, the maximum permissible LTV is capped at 80%. For valuations between ₹30 Lakhs and ₹75 Lakhs, it is capped at 75%, and luxury segments exceeding ₹75 Lakhs are restricted to a maximum 70% LTV.
* **Collateral Legal Clearances:** The underlying property must have a fully unencumbered title. Agricultural land conversions require a certified Collector's NOC, and mixed-use commercial/residential structures cannot be leveraged under standard retail books.

## SECTION 3: REPAYMENT COMPLIANCE & EMI CALCULATION RULES
* **Amortization & NACH Mandates:** All CRED retail lending instruments operate on a fixed monthly cycle where EMIs are programmatically pulled via NACH or ECS mandates on the 5th of every calendar month. There is zero grace period allocated for interest computation.
* **Prepayment-Penalty Rules:** Members wishing to foreclose or make partial pre-payments can do so after 6 successful EMI cycles. Salaried individuals face zero prepayment penalties on floating-rate home loans, while personal loans incur a flat 2% charge on the outstanding principal balance if closed before 12 months.
