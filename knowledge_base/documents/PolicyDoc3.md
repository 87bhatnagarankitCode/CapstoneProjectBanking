# CRED LENDING OPERATIONS - SPECIALIZED PRODUCTS, KYC, AND COMPLIANCE (v1.0.0)
**Document Ref:** CRED/COMP/SPEC-2026/V1
**Classification:** Internal Compliance Manual

## SECTION 1: BUSINESS, AUTO, AND EDUCATION LENDING FRAMEWORKS
* **Business Loan & Slabs:** CRED Business Loans are extended to registered entities with a minimum vintage of 3 operational years and audited turnover > ₹50 Lakhs. Interest rate slabs are categorized dynamically from Grade A (9.5% per annum) to Grade D (14.5% per annum) based on cash flow health.
* **Auto Loan Requirements:** Auto Loans cover up to 90% of the vehicle's on-road price for new premium models. Hypothecation in favor of CRED must be recorded on the state RTO smartcard before the final loan disbursement tranche is released.
* **Education Loan Framework:** Education Loans require a co-applicant (parent/guardian) acting as a primary guarantor. Moratorium periods cover the exact course duration plus an extended 6-month buffer, with mandatory academic performance updates shared each semester.

## SECTION 2: KYC DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS & STRUCTURAL POLICIES
* **KYC Document Requirements:** Digital KYC verification is mandatory for all loan categories. Applicants must submit a valid PAN Card, Aadhaar Card for biometric or OTP-based verification, and the latest 6 months' bank statements certified via Account Aggregator frameworks.
* **Joint-Account Rules:** Joint-loan structures allow up to two co-applicants, provided they are immediate family members (spouse, parents, or siblings). The credit scores and monthly income of both individuals are pooled together to maximize the eligible loan disbursement threshold.
* **NRI-Account Eligibility:** Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) are eligible exclusively for Secured Home Loans and Education Loans. Applications must be routed via verified NRE or NRO accounts, backed by a valid overseas employment contract, continuous visa status, and a local resident co-applicant.
